Methodology

X-axis: Particle size in nanometers on a logarithmic scale, where smaller values predict faster absorption. In our 2026 measurements, formulations at or below 60nm consistently posted the strongest uptake kinetics. Y-axis: Bioavailability percentage, the fraction of an administered dose that reaches systemic circulation. Traditional CBD oil achieves only 4-8%. Figures are compiled from our own Dynamic Light Scattering (DLS) measurements under ISO 17025 conditions, published Certificates of Analysis, and pharmacokinetic literature reviewed by our research division.
